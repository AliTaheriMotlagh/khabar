# khabar — Persian news site from the @dlmehr Telegram channel

Primary site: https://khabar-ai.vercel.app (canonical). GitHub Pages builds a mirror. Channel owner: Mehrzad Samadi (https://mehrzad.ai/).

## How it fits together
- `pipeline/` (Python, httpx + bs4): crawls `t.me/s/dlmehr`, takes **Gholam's first comment** (the Persian translation, posted by a bot named «غلام», signed «داش غلام») from the post's discussion widget, reads linked tweets (api.fxtwitter.com) / pages, writes `data/posts/<id>.json`. Media is **never downloaded** — posts keep original URLs (Telegram CDN URLs are signed, refreshed on every crawl).
- `src/` (Astro 7, static): `lib/posts.ts` shapes raw posts (`kind`: story | digest | brief; English digests are hidden). Components in `src/components/`, pages in `src/pages/`, one stylesheet `src/styles/global.css` (tokens at the top, light/dark).
- `src/lib/align.ts`: word timings from a spoken clip's sound (weights fitted on the site's voices, pinned to the pauses after sentences/commas, snapped to near-silences) — no imports, `tests/align.test.mjs`.
- `src/lib/time.ts`: Tehran days, `relTime` (calendar days, not 24h blocks) and the stories (`dropCompanions`, `lastDays`, `pickStories`, `nextUnseen`) — no imports, shared by build and browser, unit-tested from Node (`tests/time.test.mjs`).
- `src/scripts/`: `core.ts` (storage, helpers) · `media.ts` (loading placeholder; walks an image's addresses — `src`, then `data-alt` — on error or when one hangs 9 s; retry; fallback box) · `game.ts` (points, badges, streaks, "new since last visit") · `social.ts` (API client) · `peek.ts` (long-press story preview, fed by `p/<id>/card.json`) · `speech.ts` (read aloud: device Persian voice, else `/api/tts`, else the downloaded on-device voice) · `voice.ts` + `voice-worker.ts` (on-device Persian voices: sherpa-onnx WASM, the catalog in `src/lib/voices.ts` — 9 speakers (Piper, Matcha, Mimic3), full + int8 builds, 48–158 MB from Hugging Face / hf-mirror.com, pinned + SHA-256-checked; stored in Cache Storage `voice-v1` by hash in 8 MB parts, so the shared runtime is downloaded once and downloads resume; one worker downloads while another speaks; a pre-picker Ganji download is moved to the new keys (`migrate`); synthesis a sentence at a time) · `voice-offer.ts` (the voice sheet `VoiceOffer.astro`: a card per speaker with a preview `public/voices/<family>.mp3`, download in the background — resumed on the next page via `voice-dl` —, use, remove; the reader's pick is `voice` = id | 'system', and a picked downloaded voice goes before the device's/server's; shown when «گوش دادن» is tapped with no voice; settings line). Adding a voice: `node scripts/voices.mjs entry|bench` (see its header) · `reader.ts` (listen — the text, not the headline; speed, text size/spacing, reading mode, time left, resume position, quote sharing) · `player.ts` (`/today/`: stories like Instagram's — one circle of the news over the 3 days the page carries, no topics (a forwarded brief stamped the same second as a story is dropped as its duplicate); what plays is the reader's setting (`story`: only unseen (default) / today / all 3 days; `order`: newest (default) / oldest first — `pickStories`, same on the rail); «همه را دیده‌ام» / «از نو» in the settings set `seen-below` (the build's newest id) / clear `seen`; unseen = not opened, not watched (1 s on screen or skipped), id above `seen-below`, as of page load, plus a `#<id>` story; with only unseen, «next» = the next unseen one, then the ones skipped at the start, then the end screen; tap or sideways swipe = next/previous story; the ⚙ in the player opens the settings, a stories change restarts the show when the sheet closes (`stories-changed` event); the home rail (`TodayRail.astro`, `renderRail` in app.ts) is one circle with the unseen count and the newest unseen story's picture (grey when nothing is unseen; no rail when the setting leaves nothing); two switches: «روایت» reads each story's text (never the headline, `sayOf` in the page) and «صدای ویدیو» plays a video's own sound — with both on, a video scene is read first, then its clip plays with sound; the end screen has a recap row of this visit's stories; preloads the next scenes and drops far-away videos) · `video.ts` (a news video made on the device, 1280×720 MP4 — «ویدیو» `[data-make-video]` in a story page's reader bar, so also on the 24-hour summary's page; sheet `VideoSheet.astro`; loaded on the first tap: branded opening + chime, the headline, chapters with each word lit as it's said, the pictures in a panel, numbers popping up as said, English names as tags, closing card with the link. The plan is card.json `video` (`videoPlan` in posts.ts: a story's text then «چرا مهمه؟»; the summary one chapter per item with the linked story's pictures), indices into card.json's spoken lines, as /api/tts. The voice must be one the page can keep (`recordableVoice`/`lineAudio` in speech.ts: downloaded or cloud) — without one the voice sheet is offered first; words timed from the sound by `src/lib/align.ts`. WebCodecs + `mediabunny` (pinned) faster than real time, else MediaRecorder in real time (iOS < 26 has no AudioEncoder); share via `navigator.share` files or save) · `app.ts` (boot, settings, offline, load-more, header/tab bar that hide on scroll down).
- `api/` (Vercel Functions, plain ESM JS, no deps): comments, react, engage, live (presence/visits: a reader counts once per post and once for the site per 24 h — `SET NX EX` markers, counts in `h:uviews` / `visits:total`; "today" = distinct readers on the Tehran day), media (302 to a freshly signed Telegram CDN URL: `?p=<id>&i=<n>[&f=poster|preview]`; index order = `telegram._parse_message`), img (the picture itself, resized to WebP via wsrv.nl server-side, CDN-cached for good: `?u=` for twimg/telesco.pe/ytimg only, or the media params; `&w=` 320/800/1400), chat, leaders, tts (Azure Speech, Persian MP3 per paragraph of a story's card.json; 501 without `AZURE_SPEECH_KEY`/`AZURE_SPEECH_REGION`), sync (reader-triggered crawl: workflow_dispatch via `GITHUB_DISPATCH_TOKEN`, state read from GitHub's run list; 501 without it), watch (an external cron calls it every minute: starts a crawl when the channel has a new post — once Gholam has commented, or after 3 min — or a late Gholam comment; stateless: t.me's page vs GitHub's newest run; `WATCH_SECRET`). Open pages poll the static `build.json` (newest listed id + count, also on `<html data-build-*>`) to offer «خبرهای تازه رسید». Storage = Upstash Redis REST (`KV_REST_API_URL`/`KV_REST_API_TOKEN`); `_lib.js` has an in-memory store for tests. Files starting with `_` are not routes.
- Tags: `shape.TOPICS` keywords, whole-word in both languages, links stripped, scored (title ×3, needs 2 points, max 3, most relevant first = the section; a later tag must be in the title or have half the top score). On a topic page the card's kicker is that topic. After changing them run `--refresh` and check the diff. Gholam's error replies («Could not summarize…») count as no translation (`telegram.is_gholam_error`).
- `.github/workflows/site.yml`: crawl every 20 min → commit `data/` → build → deploy Pages (build/deploy skipped when the crawl found nothing new). Vercel redeploys on each push to main.

## Commands
- `npm run build` (astro + pagefind) · `npm run serve` (built site + API with in-memory store, port 4322)
- `npm test` (API, dates, and — after a build — the built timeline/stories vs `data/`: `tests/site.test.mjs`) · `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q pipeline/tests`
- Screenshots / UI checks: `playwright` is a devDependency (`npx playwright install chromium webkit` for its browsers — its CDN is geo-blocked here; launch Chrome with `executablePath: '/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome'`, an older cached WebKit build works via `executablePath`). WebKit needs `launchPersistentContext`: a throwaway profile drops Cache Storage on navigation. Real iOS Safari: the Simulator + `ios_webkit_debug_proxy -s unix:<webinspectord_sim.socket>` (WebKit protocol: `Runtime.evaluate` with `emulateUserGesture`, then `Runtime.awaitPromise`). Run against `npm run serve`.
- `npx astro check` (types) · `.venv/bin/python -m pipeline.run` (crawl; `PAGES=10` to backfill, `--refresh` to re-derive articles)
- Crawl into a scratch dir instead of `data/`: `DATA_DIR=/abs/path .venv/bin/python -m pipeline.run`

## Gotchas
- Readers are mostly in Iran: Telegram/X CDNs and t.me are filtered, and a browser's multiplexed connection to other foreign hosts stalls (wsrv.nl/Cloudflare: 9 parallel requests on one HTTP/2 connection → nothing; the site's Vercel domain → fine). So images come through the site first: `picture()`/`pic()` in `src/lib/posts.ts` give `src` + `data-alt` = [/api/img relay, wsrv.nl, original, fresh]. Social cards and JSON-LD keep original URLs; RSS (`src/pages/rss.xml.ts`) uses `feedPicture()` = the relay on the primary domain (readers keep items for good, and Telegram URLs expire), checked by `tests/site.test.mjs`.
- `data-fresh` is the «خبرهای تازه رسید» pill (social.ts binds a reload to every `[data-fresh]`): media use `data-alt`.
- The on-device voice: iOS audio must start in the tap — `Speaker` plays 50 ms of silence in it, then the clips. The worker is terminated on `pagehide` (Safari's back cache would keep its ~550 MB alive). Private browsing keeps no Cache Storage across pages (the sheet says so). `public/sw.js` must keep `voice-v1`.
- Telegram comment HTML has a hidden reply template with a *truncated* copy of the text — always read `.js-message_text` (see `telegram._own_text`).
- X video CDN returns 403 with a foreign Referer → site-wide `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`; outbound links to t.me/mehrzad.ai use `referrerpolicy="origin"`.
- `post.cover` is always a still image (video → poster); use `sized()` for pbs.twimg.com sizes.
- iOS has no Persian TTS voice, and only starts audio inside a tap: `Speaker.start()`/`resume()` must not await before playing.
- Safari sizes a height-less column flexbox from `flex-basis`: `flex: 1` (basis 0%) collapses it — use `flex: 1 1 auto` (see `.peek-scroll`).
- `data-today` / `data-total` / `data-online` are filled by `social.ts` with live counts site-wide: don't reuse those attribute names for anything else.
- Upstash free tier is ~500K commands/month: keep API calls batched (`engage` = one pipeline per page) and CDN-cacheable.

## Working efficiently here
- Don't read `data/posts/*.json` wholesale (60+ files); grep or read one. Don't read `package-lock.json`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`.
- When checking a build, tail the log (`npm run build 2>&1 | tail`), don't dump it.
